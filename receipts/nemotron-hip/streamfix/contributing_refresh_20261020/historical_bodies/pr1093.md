## What this changes

Tensor parallelism for the Nemotron HIP engine:

- Each rank holds its cut of the weights: the routed experts, heads and vocabulary by rank, the MTP head whole on rank 0. Every rank sums the shares in fp32 at the same places.
- Rank 0's lane backend sends every prompt pass, chunk, window, keep, page swap and snapshot slot over the link (the `hip_serve` protocol, [fork #29][f29]). A follower runs the same steps on its own shards, so the collectives pair up, and a resumed prompt or a rolled-back draft is exact on every rank.
- The ranks start through `hip.Startup`'s steps over the link. A rank's refusal stops every rank, with its reason.
- `tf-nemotron-hip-test` joins a group for teacher, window, replay and a per-block residual trace.

Family code only: `zig/src/families/nemotron/hip*` and `zig/tests/nemotron_hip`.

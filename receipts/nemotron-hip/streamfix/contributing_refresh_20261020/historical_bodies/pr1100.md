## What this changes

- `tf_argmax_part` takes the best of each 1,024-value part of a row on its own block. `tf_argmax_join` takes the best of those.
- `tf_argmax`'s order is total: a NaN first, then the larger value, then the lower index. So the best of the parts' bests is `tf_argmax`'s pick, whatever the parts are.
- `ops.argmaxRows` takes the parts for rows of 2,048 values and more while the merged launches are on. That is every HIP family's greedy draw and Nemotron's draft head.

Shared code: `ops/draw.hpp`, `launches/draw.zig`, `ops/draw.zig`, `launches.zig`. This changes Qwen3.5's and Flash Next's greedy draws: the picks are the same, as the decode logits, lanes and hashes below show.

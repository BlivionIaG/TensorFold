## What this changes

The serving glue that a HIP family needs beside its forward moves from Qwen3.5's engine into `zig/src/hip_serve`, as generic pieces:

- `backend.zig`: the stream map, the drawn-token ring, the first draws, the held drafts, the prompt pass loop, and the timed verify, keep and draft callbacks;
- `pages.zig` and `snapshots.zig`: page ids, holders and snapshot slots;
- `prefix.zig`: the prompt cache's HIP side over core `prompt_radix` (rank 0 decides, the other ranks apply);
- `protocol.zig`: the tensor-parallel step words, rank 0's sender and the follower loop;
- `costs.zig` (second commit): the lane cost timing at load.

Qwen3.5 now uses these pieces. It keeps its caches, its pool buffers, its batched verify and its draft head. Flash Next keeps its own copies. The next family (Nemotron, later in this stack) uses the same pieces.

Shared code: `hip_serve` and the Qwen3.5 HIP engine. Exactness first: the bits and the behaviour do not change.

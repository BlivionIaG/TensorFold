## What this changes

The native HIP server ignored `--slide`, and `--mtp-confidence` on a family whose drafts take no confidence. A HIP family now names its refusals with `slide_refusal` and `confidence_refusal` declarations. `open` and `follow` refuse with that text before the family loads, on every rank. Qwen3.5 and Flash Next declare neither, so they serve as before. Nemotron (later in this stack) declares both.

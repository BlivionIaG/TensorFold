## What this changes

Nemotron 3.5 Lightning 30B-A3B (`nemotron_h`, the MLX affine 4-bit checkpoint with its MTP head) on the native HIP engine, on one GPU:

- Admission reads the config, the index and the MTP file before anything loads. It counts a rank's weight bytes, a stream's keys, values and Mamba state, and the window a card holds. It refuses with a reason when the model does not fit, and it refuses by name the NVFP4/ModelOpt export, Living Weights files, pocket neurons, `--slide` and `--mtp-confidence`.
- The loader puts the weights on the device as stored. It does not convert weights in memory. The config, the draft ids and the tensor names are main's (`families/nemotron/config.zig`).
- The forward runs the Mamba-2 mixer ([fork #31][f31]), the MoE layer ([fork #33][f33]) and attention over a page pool.
- The lane backend runs on the `hip_serve` pieces ([fork #29][f29]): MTP drafts, one Mamba snapshot a row so a rejected draft rolls back exactly, and the prompt cache over core `prompt_radix`.
- `tf-nemotron-hip-test` (load, greedy, teacher-forced, window and chunk-cut checks, the replay of a served trace).

Family code only: `zig/src/families/nemotron/hip*`, its fixtures and `zig/tests/nemotron_hip`. Shared files take registration lines only: the module and the test tool in `zig/build/hip.zig`; the import, the registry entry and two test lines in `zig/src/native/hip.zig`. `--tp` is refused here; [fork #36][f36] adds it.
